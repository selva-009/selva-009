## What I've built

**[Crsek-09](https://github.com/selva-009/crsek-09)** — Real-time multiplayer squad cricket in the browser.
Duo and Squad modes where teammates share the bat, the ball and the field, on a 3D stadium with an
authoritative match engine and peer-to-peer play.
`Three.js` `JavaScript` `WebGL` `Multiplayer`

**[CleanCity Tracker](https://github.com/selva-009/cleancity-tracker)** — Municipal waste transparency and accountability.
Citizens report waste issues, the system routes them, officials act, and results are verified in the
open. Report → Track → Act → Verify → Improve.
`FastAPI` `SQLite` `Role-based access` `Audit trail`

**[Aarogya Saathi](https://github.com/selva-009/AarogyaSaathi)** — Your AI care companion.
Turns a hospital discharge summary into a plain-language, multilingual, voice-first daily checklist —
then quizzes the patient to verify they actually understood it. Built for Innovator's League 2026.
`Healthcare` `Multilingual` `Voice-first`

**[RaktSethu](https://github.com/selva-009/raktsethu)** — Thalassemia blood support system.
Connects patients with compatible donors, doctors and diagnostic labs, with a server-side
compatibility matrix, donor eligibility tracking and a 12-hour response deadline.
`PHP` `MySQL` `Matching engine`

**[SignBridge](https://github.com/selva-009/signbridge)** — Real-time sign language to text and speech, in the browser.
Camera-tracked hand landmarks, a searchable sign library, and a practice mode that scores your
gesture against a target sign.
`MediaPipe` `JavaScript` `Computer vision`

**[Aegis](https://github.com/selva-009/aegis)** — Personal safety for everyone.
A button, a shake, or a hand sign you choose. Built to be universal on purpose, because a safety
tool framed for one group only gets installed by people who already feel at risk.
`JavaScript` `Accessibility`
